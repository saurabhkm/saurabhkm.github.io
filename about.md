# Bio

I'm a Researcher at Samsung R&D as part of the Computational Imaging Group.

Before that, I did a Postdoc with [Intelligence and Sensing Lab](https://www.is.ids.osaka-u.ac.jp/en/) at Osaka University, working on Computational 3D Sensing and Computational Optics for Computer Vision.

My Ph.D. was in Computer Imaging and Vision; I was part of the [Vision and Image Processing Group](https://www.ee.iitb.ac.in/~viplab/) at IIT Bombay, supervised by
[Subhasis Chaudhuri](http://www.ee.iitb.ac.in/~sc).

## Research Interests
- Intelligent Data Acquisition
- Computational Imaging and Vision
- Scientific Machine Learning
- Resource-efficient Training and Inference

## Education
- Ph.D., [_Department of Electrical Engineering_](http://www.ee.iitb.ac.in/), [IIT Bombay](http://www.iitb.ac.in/), 2017 - 2021.
- M.Tech. (transferred to Ph.D.), _Communication and Signal Processing_, [IIT Bombay](http://www.iitb.ac.in/), 2014-17.
- B.Tech., _Electronics and Communication Engineering_, [NITK Surathkal](https://www.nitk.ac.in/), 2007-11.
